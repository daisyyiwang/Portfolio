<Quote emoji="🛒">"I have to keep an eye on her to make sure she stays in the cart."</Quote>

A customer told me this on one of my store visits, while doing her best to keep her daughter quiet and safe in the cart. According to Kohl's data, she represents about 53% of customers, who shop in store with kids. Clearly, there's a challenge here.

<Section eyebrow="My role" title="An independent internship project">
  <p>Kohl's ZzzZoo was an independent project I led during my internship at Kohl's headquarters in Milwaukee — from ideation and UX research to wireframes, prototyping and presentation. It was a great way to learn how today's retail and eCommerce world works.</p>
</Section>

<Section eyebrow="Research insights" title="Key design rationale">
  <h3>In-store shopping can't simply be replaced by online</h3>
  <Stats items={[
    { value: '78%', label: 'of shoppers are in-store only' },
    { value: '64%', label: 'of total sales come from in-store-only customers' },
    { value: '72%', label: 'of sales come from in-store purchases' },
  ]} />
  <p>For confidentiality, only some actual values are shared.</p>
  <h3>Families choose Kohl's, and Kohl's chooses families</h3>
  <p>Over half of Kohl's customers shop with kids very often.</p>
  <h3>Kids have a huge impact on the shopping experience</h3>
  <p>And the only thing they interact with is the shopping cart.</p>
</Section>

<Section eyebrow="Testing & iteration" title="There is no end in design">
  <Gallery items={[
    { src: '/images/zz-testing01.svg', alt: 'Core flow of the paper prototype: play as guest or log in via the Kohl\'s app, then explore animals.' },
    { src: '/images/zz-testing02.svg', alt: 'Updated paper prototype flow after RITE testing.' },
  ]} />
  <p>Using the RITE method, I tested the core flow with a paper prototype, sorting issues into four groups to iterate quickly: obvious cause and quick fix; obvious cause but hard fix; no obvious cause or solution; and issues caused by other factors.</p>
</Section>

<Section eyebrow="Strategy design" title="Working with data in mind">
  <p>As interest in ZzzZoo grew, one question kept coming up: what's the business value? How much could this concept bring in?</p>
  <p>Estimating business impact this early is always hard, but working with design strategy and the business data team, I estimated the sales increase using the latest traffic and conversion data from 5 sample stores, deliberately conservatively. That extra mile paid off when several teams later asked for walkthroughs of the concept.</p>
</Section>
