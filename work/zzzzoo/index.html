Kohl's ZzzZoo — a kid-friendly ecosystem

Upgrade the family-based shopping experience.

  • B2C
  • Concept
Role
Experience designer (intern)
Timeline
Jun – Aug 2016
Audience
In-store families · Tablet
🛒

“I have to keep an eye on her to make sure she stays in the cart.”

A customer told me this on one of my store visits, while doing her best to keep her daughter quiet and safe in the cart. According to Kohl’s data, she represents about 53% of customers, who shop in store with kids. Clearly, there’s a challenge here.

My role

An independent internship project

Kohl’s ZzzZoo was an independent project I led during my internship at Kohl’s headquarters in Milwaukee — from ideation and UX research to wireframes, prototyping and presentation. It was a great way to learn how today’s retail and eCommerce world works.

Design process

Observe, listen and empathize

From 6 in-store observations across 5 stores and 11 interviews with customers and associates, I created a persona, Becky, and her experience map to highlight the key findings.

🔍
  • Most parents get a cart if their kid fits in it.
  • Kids love having a cart to slide and shop around.
  • When the wait is too long, kids get restless.
  • Parents are unlikely to use the fitting room.

Data analysis

Propose an upgraded experience

With the first round of research in hand, I used an affinity diagram and the Elito method to build the design and make key decisions about the flow and interaction details.

Design ideation

Storyboard sketching

I like storyboards for connecting the dots between research and the concepts in my head — especially on projects that need early buy-in. They bring your audience into users’ daily lives and spark a real, iterative conversation about improving the in-store experience.

Testing & iteration

There is no end in design

Using the RITE method, I tested the core flow with a paper prototype, sorting issues into four groups to iterate quickly: obvious cause and quick fix; obvious cause but hard fix; no obvious cause or solution; and issues caused by other factors.

Strategy design

Working with data in mind

As interest in ZzzZoo grew, one question kept coming up: what’s the business value? How much could this concept bring in?

Estimating business impact this early is always hard, but working with design strategy and the business data team, I estimated the sales increase using the latest traffic and conversion data from 5 sample stores, deliberately conservatively. That extra mile paid off when several teams later asked for walkthroughs of the concept.
